**Promotional Text** (170 chars):

**Description:**
Strengthen your deen through daily action.

Adat is a group-first Islamic habit tracker stripped of all visual noise. Track salah, Quran reading, adhkar, sadaqah, studying, and other daily habits in one clean, focused view.

KEY FEATURES

- Today View — See only the habits due today, with no feed, no clutter, and no endless scrolling.
- Dhikr Counter — A tactile counter designed for quick, calm repetition that stays out of your way.
- Private Groups — Create small invite-only circles with close friends and stay consistent together without public noise.
- Streaks & Insights — Track streaks, view completion rates, and review private progress charts to notice patterns without feeling overwhelmed.
- Daily Quotes — Start your day with an inspiring Islamic reminder.
- Reminders — Set custom daily reminders so you never miss a habit.

OFFLINE-FIRST

Adat works fully offline. Your habit data is stored on your device first. Sign in to sync across devices and join groups — or stay as a guest and track privately.

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT

Your data is encrypted in transit and private by default. Group members only see what you choose to share inside each group. No social feed, no likes, no comments.

LANGUAGES

Available in English, Russian, and Kazakh.

Adat is free. The core habit tracking and group features will always remain free.
